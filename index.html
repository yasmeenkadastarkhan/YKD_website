What we cook

Fresh, halal and made in small batches in a certified home kitchen.

  • Desi meals

    Biryani, karahi, curries, daal chawal, sabzi roti. Real spices, quality ingredients.

  • Street food & fusion

    Chaat, samosas, rolls and Indo-Pak favourites with a modern twist.

  • Desserts & drinks

    Gulab jamun, kheer, rasmalai, mango lassi, masala chai and rose milk.

  • Bulk cooking

    Homemade halal dishes for religious events, community gatherings, festivals, ceremonies, corporate events and family occasions.

  • Home cooking

    Freshly prepared meals for your household, made with care and your preferences in mind.

  • On-site cooking

    Yasmeen cooks at your home or event location using your kitchen and equipment, then cleans the cooking area afterward.
